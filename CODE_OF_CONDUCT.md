# Community Code of Conduct

We want Overlay's community to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.

Participants must be respectful, assume good faith while evaluating evidence,
accept technical disagreement without personal attacks, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, or disclosure of another
person's private information.

Security reports, customer data, credentials, and private communications must
not be posted publicly. Follow `SECURITY.md` for vulnerability reporting.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms people, the project, or coordinated security work. Enforcement
reports may be sent privately to divyansh@layernorm.co. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical; conflicts of interest should be disclosed so a
different reviewer can handle the report.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project. Good-faith criticism of code, architecture, product decisions, and
maintainer actions is allowed and encouraged when it remains focused on the
work.
