They are included for nominative interface use only. No endorsement is implied.

## File-type icons and other interface assets

Before an official public binary release, the release owner must confirm and
record the source and redistribution terms for every non-first-party image,
sound, and icon in `resources/` and `public/`. Unknown provenance blocks the
release; it must not be papered over with an assumed license.
